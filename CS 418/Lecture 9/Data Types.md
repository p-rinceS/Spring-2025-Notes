How to [visualize]([[visualizing]])
Quantitative Data:
	Histograms, Box Plots, Rug Plots, Soothed Interpolations.
	<font color="#4f81bd">Look for:</font> spread, shape, modes, outliers, unreasonable values.
Categorical Data (Nominal & Ordinal):
	Bar Plots
	<font color="#4f81bd">Look for:</font> skew, frequent and rare categories, or invalid categories.
	Consider <font color="#f79646">grouping</font> categories and repeating analysis.
