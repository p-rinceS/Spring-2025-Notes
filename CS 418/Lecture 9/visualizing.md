
Visualization helps to quickly infer things about the data.

For example, if you are using a bar graph and see that the second bar is double than the first bar, you can infer that burglary occurs twice as often as vandalism.

## Why should we do visualization

- Data exploration visualization:
	- Figuring out what is true
- Data presentation visualization:
	- Convincing other people it is true

"Data exploration" is much broader than just visualization.


## Importance

<font color="#4bacc6">Data Cleaning</font> : Visualization gives you ideas on how to represent or clean your data better.
<font color="#4bacc6">Performance</font> : For the performance of ML algorithms, the right pre-processing and cleaning can be more important than which specific algorithm you choose.
