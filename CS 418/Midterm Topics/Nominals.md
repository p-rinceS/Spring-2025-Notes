Another term for categorical data without an inherent order.


| **Type**                            | **Definition**                    | **Examples**                                          |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Quantitative (Numerical)**        | Measurable, can do math with it   | Age (25), Height (5’8”), Salary ($50K)                |
| **Qualitative (Categorical)**       | Descriptive, not numerical        | Eye Color (Blue), Gender (Male)                       |
| **Nominal (Subset of Categorical)** | No natural order                  | Car brands (Ford, Honda, Toyota)                      |
| **Ordinal (Subset of Categorical)** | Ordered but no precise difference | Education Level (High School < Bachelor's < Master's) |
