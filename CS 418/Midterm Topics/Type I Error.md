

### **Medical Testing Example** (Disease Diagnosis)

- **[[Null Hypothesis]] (H₀):** The patient does **not** have cancer.
- **[[Alternate Hypothesis]] (H₁):** The patient **has** cancer.


**[[Type I Error]] (False Positive)** → The test **incorrectly detects** cancer in a healthy patient.

- Consequence: The person goes through **unnecessary** treatments (e.g., chemotherapy).

**[[Type II Error]] (False Negative)** → The test **fails to detect** cancer in a sick patient.

- Consequence: The person does **not get treatment**, and the disease worsens.
