A default assumption that no relationship exists between variables.


A pharmaceutical company is testing whether a new drug lowers blood pressure.

- **Null Hypothesis (H₀)**: The new drug has **no effect** on blood pressure.
    
    - (Mathematically: **μ₁ = μ₂**, meaning the average blood pressure before and after taking the drug is the same.)


- **[[Alternate Hypothesis]] (H₁)**: The new drug **lowers blood pressure**.
    
    - (Mathematically: **μ₁ > μ₂**, meaning the average blood pressure after taking the drug is lower.)


1. **Students' Test Scores**
    
    - H₀: Studying **at night** has **no effect** on test scores.
    - H₁: Studying at night **improves** test scores.
2. **Website Redesign and Sales**
    
    - H₀: A new website design **does not change** sales.
    - H₁: A new website design **increases** sales.

	The null hypothesis always assumes **no effect, no difference, or no relationship**, while the alternate hypothesis suggests **a change or difference**.
