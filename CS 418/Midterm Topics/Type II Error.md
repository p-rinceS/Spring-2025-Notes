### **Courtroom Example** (Guilt vs. Innocence)

- **Null Hypothesis (H₀):** The defendant is **innocent**.
- **Alternate Hypothesis (H₁):** The defendant is **guilty**.

 **[[Type I Error]] (False Positive)** → Convicting an innocent person.

- Consequence: An innocent person **goes to jail**.

 **[[Type II Error]] (False Negative)** → Letting a guilty person go free.

- Consequence: A guilty person **escapes punishment** and may commit more crimes.
