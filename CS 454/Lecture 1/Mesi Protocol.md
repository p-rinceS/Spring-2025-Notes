Invalidate-based [[Cache Coherence]] protocol and it's one of the most common protocols that support write-back caches. 
- AKA as the Illinois Protocol because it was developed at U of I.


The letters in the acronym represent four exclusive states that a cache line can be marked with (encoded using two additional bits)
